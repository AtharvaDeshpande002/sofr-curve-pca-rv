SOFR curve PCA & butterfly mean reversion

PCA rich/cheap model of the SOFR futures curve, 2018-06-01 to 2026-10-19. Walk-forward backtest of fly and double-fly mean reversion: 361 trades, gross Sharpe 0.49, net Sharpe -6.27 after costs. The signal is real but smaller than the cost of crossing every leg.

1. The answer in one picture

Average move back toward fair value after a signal, by size of the signal, versus the cost of trading one unit. Out-of-sample z-scores only.

2. PCA of daily curve changes

Positions 1-12 = the next twelve quarterly SR3 contracts. Daily changes are computed on fixed contracts, so quarterly rolls never create false moves.

3. Are the factors stable?

Loadings estimated separately in each policy period, and the explained variance on a rolling 252-day window.

4. Rich/cheap through time

z-score of each fly's residual from the curve's level/slope/curvature, from the walk-forward model in force on that day.

5. Worked example

FLY 5-6-7 over the last 504 sessions, with the out-of-sample trades the rule took.

6. Mean-reversion speed

Median AR(1) half-life of each residual across the walk-forward training windows.

7. Walk-forward backtest

Fly and double-fly book, one unit per trade, signal at the close of t, trade at the settle of t+1, at most 2 open trades per curve region.
